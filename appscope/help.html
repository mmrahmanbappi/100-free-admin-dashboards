Questions

How do I invite my team?

Open Team, click Invite member and enter their email. They get a link to set a password.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Every list has an Export button that downloads a spreadsheet.

How do I switch to dark mode?

Click the moon button at the top of any page. Your choice is remembered.

Still stuck?

Our support team usually replies within a few hours on working days.
