Assets NOT included
-------------------
No photography, no icon font and no third party icon set is used. Every icon is
inline SVG authored for this template. Product and customer images are generated
from initials in CSS, so there is nothing to license and nothing to replace.

Demo content
------------
Every store name, product, customer, staff member, order, email address and
domain in the demo data is invented. Any resemblance to a real business is
coincidental.
