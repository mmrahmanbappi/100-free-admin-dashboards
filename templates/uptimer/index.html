What you can manage

  • Servers – Every machine you watch.
  • Incidents – Outages and slowdowns.
  • Alerts – Rules that page your team.
  • Services – The services that make up your app.
  • Uptime checks – Pings from around the world.

At a glance on the overview page

  • Uptime this month
  • Servers online

Extra tools

  • Live status – Real-time health of every service.
  • Incident board – Incidents from alert to post-mortem.
  • Performance – Latency, errors and throughput.
  • Regions – Servers and traffic by region.
  • On-call – Who is on call and when.
  • Incident timeline – What happened during the last outage.
  • Notification rules – Who gets woken up, and when.

FAQ

Questions about this template

Is this dashboard free to use?

Yes. It uses the MIT license, so you can use it in personal, client and commercial projects at no cost.

Does it need a framework or build step?

No. It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Open a page in a browser, or upload the folder to any host.

Can I connect it to my own data?

The pages use realistic sample data so you can see every screen. Replace it with your own API or database when you build your app.

Can I change the colors and text?

Yes. Colors are CSS variables at the top of each stylesheet, and all text is plain HTML you can edit directly.
